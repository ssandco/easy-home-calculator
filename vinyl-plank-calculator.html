Common questions

Straight answers, in plain words.

How much vinyl plank flooring do I need?

Enter your measurements in the Vinyl Plank Flooring Calculator above — it uses this formula: Boxes = room area × 1.10 ÷ coverage per box. Diagonal layouts need 15%. The result is rounded up, so you buy whole bags, boxes or gallons and do not run short mid-job.

How do I calculate vinyl plank flooring?

Boxes = room area × 1.10 ÷ coverage per box. Diagonal layouts need 15%. Measure in feet (depth in inches, as the calculator asks), then divide by how much one bag, gallon, box or roll covers. Add 10% extra for cuts and waste — the calculator includes a waste allowance you can change.

How accurate is this vinyl plank flooring calculator?

It is an estimate for planning, using standard US product yields — for example one gallon of paint covers about 350 sq ft per coat and an 80 lb bag of concrete makes about 0.6 cu ft. Always check the label on the product you buy and your local building code before ordering.
