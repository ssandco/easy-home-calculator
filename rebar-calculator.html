Common questions

Straight answers, in plain words.

How much rebar do I need?

Enter your measurements in the Rebar Calculator above — it uses this formula: Bars each way = (other side ÷ spacing) + 1. Total feet = bars × lengths. Sticks = total ÷ 20 ft. The result is rounded up, so you buy whole bags, boxes or gallons and do not run short mid-job.

How do I calculate rebar?

Bars each way = (other side ÷ spacing) + 1. Total feet = bars × lengths. Sticks = total ÷ 20 ft. Measure in feet (depth in inches, as the calculator asks), then divide by how much one bag, gallon, box or roll covers. Add 10% extra for cuts and waste — the calculator includes a waste allowance you can change.

How accurate is this rebar calculator?

It is an estimate for planning, using standard US product yields — for example one gallon of paint covers about 350 sq ft per coat and an 80 lb bag of concrete makes about 0.6 cu ft. Always check the label on the product you buy and your local building code before ordering.
